The Shattered Crown

When kingdoms fall, heroes rise from the ashes

⌄

Campaign Synopsis

The ancient kingdom of Aeldoria lies in ruins after the mysterious disappearance of King Aldric and the shattering of the Crown of Unity. Without the crown's magic, the land fragments—literally and politically. Rival nobles claim territories, strange rifts tear through reality, and whispers speak of an ancient evil awakening in the north. You are strangers brought together by fate in the border town of Thornhaven, each carrying your own reasons to seek the crown's fragments. The path ahead is perilous, but legends say those who unite the crown shall command its power.

Dark omens plague the land: crops wither, the dead walk, and reality itself seems unstable. The Church of the Celestial Order maintains that only through divine intervention can order be restored, while the Mage Conclave believes arcane knowledge holds the key. Meanwhile, common folk suffer as warlords carve out petty kingdoms from Aeldoria's corpse.

Starting Location

🗺️

Thornhaven

A fortified border town nestled between the Whisperwood Forest and the Ironpeak Mountains. Once a prosperous trading post, Thornhaven now serves as a refuge for those fleeing the chaos engulfing Aeldoria. The town is ruled by Lady Merideth, a pragmatic leader who maintains uneasy neutrality between warring factions. Strange occurrences have plagued the town recently—disappearances in the forest, unnatural storms, and reports of spectral lights in the old ruins to the north.

Known NPCs

👑

Lady Merideth Thornwood

Ruler of Thornhaven

A sharp-minded noble in her forties who inherited Thornhaven from her father. She walks a diplomatic tightrope, keeping the town independent while rival lords circle like vultures. Known for her silver tongue and network of spies.

🧙

Aldren the Keeper

Town Sage & Historian

An elderly scholar who maintains Thornhaven's library and serves as advisor to Lady Merideth. He possesses extensive knowledge of Aeldoria's history and the Crown of Unity. His hands shake from a mysterious affliction.

⚔️

Captain Garren Blackstone

Commander of the Town Guard

A grizzled veteran of the kingdom's last wars. Loyal to a fault and fiercely protective of Thornhaven's people. He maintains strict watch over the town walls and organizes patrols into the surrounding wilderness.

Player Information

Campaign Tone & Themes

This is a dark fantasy campaign balancing serious themes with moments of heroism and hope. Expect political intrigue, moral dilemmas, and high-stakes combat. The world is dangerous but not grimdark—heroes can make a difference. Themes include power and corruption, the cost of war, redemption, and the nature of unity.

House Rules & Expectations

We use milestone leveling. Flanking grants advantage. Healing potions are bonus actions. Session length: 3-4 hours. Please arrive on time and stay engaged. Respect other players' boundaries and use safety tools. Character creation is point buy, all official books allowed with DM approval.

Session Zero Notes

Before we begin, we'll establish group expectations, discuss lines and veils, create characters together, and establish party bonds. Bring 2-3 character concepts. We'll also discuss scheduling, communication preferences, and any accessibility needs. First session will be character-focused roleplay in Thornhaven.
